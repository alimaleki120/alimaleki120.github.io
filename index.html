01

Hydrodynamic modeling of Mobile Bay

Developing and calibrating an EFDC+ model to investigate water-level dynamics in a shallow estuary on the northern Gulf Coast.

EFDC+Model calibrationWater levels
Explore the project

The question. How do model configuration and boundary conditions affect simulated water levels across Mobile Bay?

The approach. Compare simulations with station observations and examine multiple performance measures to understand bias, timing, and variability. Evaluate boundary choices, roughness, domain configuration, and turbulence settings.

02

Wind sensitivity in an estuarine system

Investigating how wind speed and direction influence simulated water levels under contrasting seasonal conditions.

Sensitivity analysisWind forcingExperimental design
Explore the project

The question. How sensitive is the modeled estuary to different wind conditions?

The approach. Use a structured set of wind-speed and direction scenarios to compare water-level responses, including peaks, ranges, and variability, across seasonal study periods.

03

Environmental conditions & oyster mortality

Exploring water-quality conditions associated with Sudden Unusual Mortality Syndrome (SUMS) in oysters.

Water qualityEvent-based analysisCoastal aquaculture
Explore the project

The question. What environmental patterns occur before and during documented oyster mortality events?

The approach. Summarize temperature, salinity, and dissolved oxygen exposure and compare event periods with reference periods. Account for data coverage and the limited number of independent events when interpreting associations.
